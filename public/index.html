How to play

  1. Drag from a dot to the other dot of the same colour to lay a line.
  2. Lines can't cross or branch. Draw over another line to cut it back.
  3. The board is solved when every pair is linked and every square is covered.
  4. The daily line is the same for everyone and grows from 7 × 7 on Monday to 11 × 11 at the weekend. Solve it each day to keep your streak.
  5. Perfect means you finished with one move per colour and no hints.

Tap ABC to put letters on the dots if colours are hard to tell apart.
